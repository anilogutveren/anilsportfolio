Practice, groove, and consistency: my drumming routine

  • Anil Ogutveren
  • Drumming

I have been playing drums for 5 years with Goodbye Prophecy, a German band where I perform regularly.

I also play in my second band, Hexistenz, where we create Turkish metal and rock music. This page collects highlights from both projects, live sessions, and ongoing practice routines.
